    About PerpPilot

    PerpPilot is a copy-trading co-pilot for Hyperliquid perpetuals built on the Nansen API for the Meridian Buildathon. It finds the most profitable perps traders, tracks their live positions, scores their risk, and alerts you the moment they open, close, flip, add, reduce — or get close to liquidation.

    Nansen endpoints used

    • POST /api/v1/perp-leaderboard — discover top traders (Smart HL Perps Trader cohort, 7d)
    • POST /api/v1/profiler/perp-positions — live positions per tracked wallet (entry / liq / leverage / uPnL)
    • POST /api/v1/profiler/perp-trades — 30d trade history → win rate, profit factor, equity curve
    • POST /api/v1/smart-money/perp-trades — smart money trade firehose

    Risk score

    Composite of max leverage, closest liquidation distance, position concentration and margin usage → LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH.

    Consensus & confluence

    The consensus strip aggregates live positioning across your whole watchlist per coin. A confluence alert fires when 2+ tracked traders open the same side of the same coin within 15 minutes — a flock signal worth copying.

    Alert webhooks

    Paste a Discord or Telegram bot webhook to push alerts to your phone. Telegram format: https://api.telegram.org/bot<token>/sendMessage + chat id.
