# Brag Plan: PerpPilot

## What is this app?
PerpPilot is a Hyperliquid copy-trading co-pilot built on the Nansen API: it ranks the most profitable perps traders, scores who is actually worth copying (30d win rate, profit factor, Copy Score, risk), and alerts you live the moment a tracked whale opens, closes, or flips a position.

## The angle
The numbers brag for you. One tracked wallet is running a **$10.9M long on ZEC with +$5.8M unrealized** — and PerpPilot pings you the second that wallet blinks. Institutional trading-terminal aesthetics, degen-scale numbers.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dark terminal screen. Monospace digits count up fast and slam into **$10,934,023** with the caption "one whale's ZEC long — tracked live." The number IS the hook; nobody scrolls past ten million dollars.

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **Discover → Track**: leaderboard rows with real trader labels and 7d PnL slide in; a cursor clicks **Track** and the button flips to "Tracking".
2. **The scores**: watchlist card with **CS 59 MODERATE** Copy Score badge, **70.2% win rate**, **profit factor 1457**, red **RISK 66 HIGH** badge — all counting up.
3. **The alert**: a toast slams in — "▲ Opened SHORT $118K CC (3x)" — followed by the flock signal "⇉ 2 tracked traders opened the same side within 15 min".

## Outro / punchline
Logo lockup + "Every number is Nansen data." → PerpPilot wordmark + "Built for the Meridian Buildathon."

## User flow worth showing
Discover leaderboard → click Track → watchlist card with live scores → open trader drawer ($10.9M ZEC long, skill-by-coin) → alert fires. The centerpiece scenes recreate this working-app flow, not marketing sections.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: "institutional trading-desk reel with a degen wink — the numbers do the bragging"
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, confident restraint; monospace numerals and terminal colors; motion is precise (ticks, counters, glows), never chaotic.

## Duration: 21 seconds

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support with restrained professional accents
- Music: minimal electronic bed, low-key confidence (bundled Hyperframes track; pick at composition time)
- Music treatment: enters under the hook, swells slightly at the whale number slam, steady through mid scenes, soft fade on the outro logo
- Music cue guidance: no preset read yet — cues to be detected at composition time; target strong cues at the hook number-slam (~0:02) and the alert toast (~0:15); sequential reveals (leaderboard rows, stat count-ups) snap to the beat grid or hold per reading floors
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — hook number glow and card presence may breathe with bass; no waveform bars
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched — digit ticks on count-ups, one soft click on the Track button, one alert "ping" on the toast
- Audio-coupled moments: count-up on the $10.9M figure, Track-button click, card-by-card score reveals, toast slam
- Restraint rule: no sirens, no stock "cash register" clichés; the reel stays composed even when the numbers are absurd

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook: the whale — 3.5s
Black terminal screen with faint blue grid. Monospace digits rapid-count up and slam into **$10,934,023** (green). Caption types in under it: "one whale's ZEC long — tracked live". PerpPilot mark (gradient badge) sits top-left.
Sequential/interaction: yes — digit count-up (~1.2s), then caption types (~0.8s), both hold.
Audio intent: low confident swell; tension resolving into the slam.
Audio-coupled idea: digit ticks during count-up, deep soft hit on the slam; caption typing with faint key ticks.
Music: minimal electronic bed enters under the number.
Transition mood: clean cut → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Discover → Track — 4.5s
Recreated leaderboard panel (real labels, real scale): rows slide in one by one — "HL Perps Whale · 7d PnL +$5.72M", "cosmicvision.eth · +$2.55M", "Uses TRADEXYZ1 · +$2.47M" — each with a green/red ROI chip. A cursor moves in and clicks **Track** on the whale row; button flips to "Tracking" with a subtle glow.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 rows arrive one by one (~0.4s apart, then hold as a set); cursor click on Track button.
Audio intent: rhythm builds quietly; each row lands on or near a beat.
Audio-coupled idea: row arrival ticks; single soft click on Track; button-flip whoosh (quiet).
Transition mood: soft slide → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — The scores — 5s
Watchlist card recreation centered on screen: header "HL Perps Whale", badges **CS 59 MODERATE** and **RISK 66 HIGH** pop in, then the stat grid counts up: Win rate 70.2% · Profit factor 1457 · 30d PnL +$48.2K · sparkline draws left-to-right beneath. All numerals monospace, tabular.
Sequential/interaction: yes — badges pop, four stats count up staggered, sparkline draws.
Audio intent: confident, precise; the "due diligence" moment.
Audio-coupled idea: counter ticks per stat; subtle rise in the bed.
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — The alert — 4s
Terminal background dims; an alert toast slams in from the right: colored icon ▲, "HL Perps Whale", "Opened SHORT $118K CC (3x)" with green accent border. A second toast follows beneath: "⇉ 2 tracked traders opened the same side within 15 min — flock signal". Under them, a mini alert-feed row list echoes the types.
Sequential/interaction: yes — two toasts arrive one by one; feed rows cascade.
Audio intent: the payoff moment — a clear, single alert ping; bed lifts.
Audio-coupled idea: toast slam with one crisp ping; second toast softer double-tick.
Transition mood: dramatic wipe to black → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3.5s
Logo lockup (gradient mark + Perp**Pilot** wordmark) fades up on the terminal grid. Line: "Every number is Nansen data." Then smaller: "Hyperliquid copy-trading co-pilot · Built for the Meridian Buildathon".
Sequential/interaction: none — hold.
Audio intent: resolve; bed fades out with a soft final hit on the logo.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: fade to silence.

**Music mood for this video:** cinematic-minimal, confident
**Audio summary:** a restrained electronic bed under precise tick/counter accents, one ping payoff on the alert slam, fading out under the logo — the audio stays as composed as the terminal it lives in.
