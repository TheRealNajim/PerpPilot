==================== TWEET 2/4 — how it works (attach: docs/screenshot-dashboard.png) ====================

How it works:

The Discover tab ranks the top Hyperliquid perps traders of the past week from Nansen's Smart HL Perps Trader cohort.

One click tracks any wallet. PerpPilot then pulls 30 days of trade history and computes win rate, profit factor, equity curve and a 0-100 Copy Score — plus a live risk score from leverage, liquidation distance, concentration and margin.

==================== TWEET 3/4 — the alert layer ====================

The payoff is the alert layer.

It polls live positions and fires the moment a tracked whale opens, closes, flips, adds, reduces — or drifts near liquidation.

And when 2+ tracked traders take the same side within 15 minutes? Flock signal. On screen, or straight to your phone via Telegram/Discord webhook.
